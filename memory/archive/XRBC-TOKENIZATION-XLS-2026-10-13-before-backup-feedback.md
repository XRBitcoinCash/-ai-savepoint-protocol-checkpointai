## Accepted behavior and concrete safeguards

- The holding gate remains 2,500 XRBC. Before signing, exact decimal checks reserve every new burn: 2,500.000001 for one publication, 2,500.000004 for four. The unit stays 0.000001 per publication or revision from launch, with native XRP network fees separate. Failure of the all-or-nothing actions does not guarantee a refund of the outer XRP fee.
- Default atomic publication shares the record fingerprint and XRBC_PUBLICATION_BATCH commitment between the burn and NFT; verified ParentBatchID links them to the parent. No burn hash is guessed before signing. Existing standalone burns use XRBC_BURN_TX, retain unused-history checks, and are not silently charged again.
- The composer accepts supported record/issuer actions from the connected account, automatically adds NFT burns and shows the final count/cost. Existing-MPT authorization may precede a matching distribution; aggregate issuance/declared budgets are checked. Counterparty signing coordination remains separate; the wallet cannot silently sign another account's action. Dependent new NFT/MPT IDs cannot be guessed.
- Fresh checks require validated Mainnet identity, BatchV1_1 and fixBatchV1_2, current sequence, deadline, exact fee at or below 1 XRP, account funding/reserves, retained XRBC holdings and record/issuer permissions. Conservative reserve slots cover possible new NFT pages/issuance/holder objects. Standard simulate cannot validate a complete Batch: individual action checks and the described MPT authorization dependency are explicitly distinguished.
- The signing adapter now preserves Batch Fee and Sequence instead of removing them for wallet autofill. Exact fee, outer/inner sequences, order, flags and all reviewed fields are verified independently; shared intent hashing still treats root dynamic fields specially. Retain cross-tab locks, explicit review, Mainnet forcing, challenge/intent binding, QR/deep links, pending storage, expiry/cancel handling and no automatic replacement.
- Every inner ID is independently derived with the pinned XRPL encoder and SHA-512Half. Completion requires all validated tesSUCCESS results, the exact ParentBatchID, ordered metadata and the same ledger. Burns must show exact trustline reduction and new mints must return their NFT IDs. Outer success alone is insufficient. Source refs establish validated signer involvement/observed assets, not a full exchange/unit origin or legal title.
