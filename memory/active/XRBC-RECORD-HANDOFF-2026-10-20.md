Preserve pinned official RS256 issuer/JWKS/app/audience/account checks, origin/action/body/rate bounds, exact recipient-restricted submit=false approval, fresh mutation state and consistency checks on signed/resolved/disabled payloads. No automatic POST retry or replacement after ambiguous provider/create/cancel/submission outcomes. A cache/status response must not become signing authority.

Freeze outer submitting Account/Sequence and the exact ordered plan before the recipient signature. Retain native BatchSigner verification and fresh account/network/sequence/NFT ownership/fee/reserve checks before the deliberate sender final request. This is ledger enforcement, not a global browser reservation service. A failed competing Batch may still incur its outer XRP fee. Never claim that local locking prevents every competing Xaman request.

Recovery must preserve exact request identities and unknown states. Reconcile independently against validated transactions before clearing terminal/reservation state. A known explicit pre-dispatch rollback requires the exact no-dispatch error code, HTTP503, dispatched=false and successful own CAS release. Initial creation may release only that proven reservation; final creation restores approved state with senderRequest=null. Ambiguous responses or CAS races stay locked. Fresh review preserves the entered form. No wallet transaction is repeated to recover context; losing a page/session does not revoke recipient consent.

Completed assets/transactions are on XRPL; this journal concerns off-ledger workflow state. Raw personal records, signing keys and server credentials do not belong in it or public memory.
