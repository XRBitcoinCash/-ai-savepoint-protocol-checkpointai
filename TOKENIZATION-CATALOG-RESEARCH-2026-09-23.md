## Findings from primary sources checked 2026-09-23

- Ondo's own homepage reports **450+ assets** in Ondo Stocks, notes they are unavailable in the US, and its published stock networks are Ethereum, BNB Chain and Solana. Its count is a provider catalog snapshot, not a count of XRPL stocks. Source: https://ondo.finance/ and https://ondo.finance/ondo-stocks
- Kraken's xStocks FAQ reports **131 assets**: 100 stocks, 27 ETFs, four specialist assets, with country restrictions. It says holders receive economic exposure without shareholder voting rights or a legal claim to the underlying company shares; it lists self-custody networks Solana, Ethereum, TON and Ink. Source: https://support.kraken.com/articles/xstocks-faq
- Dinari's dShares catalog includes stocks, ETFs, BDCs and REITs, searchable by symbol/company. Dinari describes 1:1 custodial backing, a broker-dealer/custodian, redemption and corporate-action handling. Its API requires a partner workflow including identity, account, funding and order steps. Do not infer that the site can mint Dinari stock on XRPL merely from a listing. Sources: https://dinari.com/dshares and https://docs.dinari.com/docs/quickstart
- RWA.xyz has an existing tokenized-stocks discovery interface with asset metrics, CSV and filters and distinguishes native from synthetic representations. Its asset API supports filtering/pagination but requires a key and access entitlement. Integrate server-side only after terms, permission and quota review; no API key in public JavaScript. Sources: https://app.rwa.xyz/stocks and https://docs.rwa.xyz/api/quickstart
- XRPL supports NFT, classic trust-line tokens and MPTs. An MPT issuance has a unique issuance ID and on-ledger metadata/controls; account_objects and ledger_data can enumerate MPT issuance objects, but a raw ledger search does not prove that a token represents a stock. Classic token identity is issuer account plus exact currency code. Issuer domain and HTTPS xrp-ledger.toml can corroborate identity only when the ledger account Domain matches. Sources: https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens ; https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/fungible-tokens/multi-purpose-tokens ; https://xrpl.org/docs/references/http-websocket-apis/api-conventions/ledger-entry-short-names ; https://xrpl.org/docs/references/xrp-ledger-toml
- The SEC's January 2026 staff statement distinguishes issuer-sponsored, custodial third-party and synthetic tokenized securities, each with different rights and counterparty risks. The SEC's September 17, 2026 temporary conditional trading exemption is confined to qualifying tokenized NMS stocks and qualifying permissioned venues with specific conditions; it is not permission for an arbitrary site visitor to issue or publicly sell a stock token. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/corp-fin-statement-tokenized-securities-012826-statement-tokenized-securities and https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment
- Open GitHub comparisons: `mojoats/xtoken-studio-xrpl` describes XRPL MPT issuer controls and a React studio but explicitly calls itself pre-v0.1, so use it for ideas only; `nsmithau/xrpl-registrar` models issuance-centered, resumable, provenance-aware XRPL history and honest incomplete coverage; `ERC-3643/ERC-3643` shows identity/compliance registries for regulated securities on another chain and cannot be transplanted as XRPL transaction code. Repositories: https://github.com/mojoats/xtoken-studio-xrpl ; https://github.com/nsmithau/xrpl-registrar ; https://github.com/ERC-3643/ERC-3643 . No third-party code or artwork was copied.

## Product model: one catalog, two distinct actions

**Explore what exists:** a public read-only catalog, no Xaman connection or XRBC gate. Separate tabs/filters for (a) verified XRPL issuance, (b) externally issued tokenized stocks/ETFs on other networks, and (c) examples or issuer submissions pending independent verification. Search by company, ticker, issuer, asset type, issuance ID, currency code or network. Show source/date and a coverage statement such as “451+ provider-reported entries; N independently verified XRPL issuances” only after actual ingestion. Never say “all tokenized stocks” without a defined source and date. A ticker search may return multiple unrelated products; do not collapse them.

**Prepare an asset:** an issuer-side workflow, starting with “What asset or right do you control?” then “What will holders actually receive?” then evidence, registry/custody/eligibility, ledger format, review and optional signing. Choosing TSLA from the discovery list is an **example or reference**; it never auto-mints a TSLA share, uses Tesla branding as issuer authorization, or creates an unbacked equity clone. Provide “Plan a stock-backed issuance” for genuinely authorized issuers, with fields for transfer agent, legal issuer, custody, backing/reconciliation, shareholder or contractual rights, corporate actions, restrictions, redemption, jurisdiction and approved distribution path. Where issuer authority or legal/operational relationships are unverified, keep the output a private plan/evidence record.

## Catalog row and data contract

Keep a versioned normalized record with `source_id`, `source_url`, `source_checked_at`, `last_verified_at`, `asset_class`, `underlying_name/ticker`, `product_name/ticker`, `issuer_legal_name`, `rights_model` (direct/entitlement/economic exposure/unknown), `dividends`, `voting`, `custodian`, `redemption`, `jurisdiction/eligibility`, `chains[]`, `status`, `disclosure_url`, `evidence_level` and `coverage_status`. For XRPL, identify MPT by network + issuance ID; classic by network + issuer + exact currency bytes; NFT by network + NFTokenID. For EVM and other networks, use chain identifier + contract/mint identifier. Never identify solely by ticker or a logo.

Only label **verified XRPL** when validated ledger object and exact issuer identity are checked and a credible issuer source supports the represented asset/right. Distinguish “ledger-verified token” from “verified backing/legal right.” Use source-confirmed, issuer-claimed, ledger-confirmed and unverified states. Store contradictory claims, stale data and missing provider fields explicitly. Separate reference market price from token quote; no implied liquidity or trade availability. Show country restrictions based on provider terms, without claiming eligibility for the viewer.

Catalog ingestion should start with permitted APIs and issuer pages, cache snapshots with attribution, pagination and rate limits; do not scrape behind access controls. RWA.xyz can be a possible licensed enrichment source. No secret in frontend. Reconcile provider entries, never manufacture an XRPL equivalent of a token on another chain. Provide “Source unavailable” and a last-good timestamp on errors, not an empty-results claim. XRPL discoverability requires known issuer seeds, registry feeds or a bounded ledger indexer; a complete global inventory is not guaranteed by a one-shot `account_objects` call.

## Broad asset chooser

Keep categories searchable and extensible, with custom entry: public/private equity; ETFs/funds/Treasury money-market funds; bonds/commercial paper/receivables/loans; stablecoins/currencies; commodities/precious metals; real estate/title or beneficial interests; vehicles/equipment/inventory; invoices and royalties; intellectual property/licences; carbon/energy/environmental claims; event tickets/membership/credentials; collectibles and other unique property; medical/veterinary/service records and evidence receipts. Each category has a plain-language example, likely NFT vs MPT vs classic route, necessary supporting evidence, holder rights, transfer/eligibility controls, and “what this token does not prove.” Do not infer title, clinical truth or tradability from minting.

## Page treatment

- **Basic Auditor:** public Explore catalog first, then guided search/asset picker and 3–5 clear steps to a free local record. Display source/chain/status/right prominently. Let users jump to a stock or real-world asset template without typing a cryptic issuer code. Keep existing local export, wallet-free use and optional signed route.
- **Advanced Auditor:** same catalog/data source, with issuer comparison, custody and reserve evidence, legal rights matrix, jurisdiction/eligibility, cap table/holder controls, corporate actions, pricing/liquidity caveats, validated XRPL identity and Evidence Pack exports. The 2,500 XRBC gate remains on privileged signing; do not gate public market discovery. Verify gate enforcement server-side wherever privileged APIs exist.
- Progressive disclosure: simple labels up front; exact ledger IDs and transaction fields in expandable details; visible source and timestamp; keyboard/mobile table/card views; intentional pagination and “more results” with no silent cutoff.
- Do not replace the existing secure Xaman authorize/sign/validated-receipt sequence, reuse old requests, or treat a browser authorization as issuer legal authority. Check amendment and compatibility at runtime.
